ABOUT

BIO

Hi, I'm Sam Calkins and thank you for checking out my website. I am from Portland, Oregon and grew up enjoying the outdoors and everything the Pacific Northwest has to offer. I am an avid skier, surfer, and backpacker.

I graduated from the University of Oregon with a bachelor's degree in Planning, Public Policy, and Management with an emphasis on Urban Design in 2025. During my undergrad I was a member of the university's Ski Club.

After discovering the opportunity to get my master's in Sports Product Design, I moved back to Portland to pursue my degree. During my experience studying Sports Product Design, I have gotten to face the design process head on, developing research, sketching, 3D modeling, prototyping, and testing skills through my outdoor product designs.
